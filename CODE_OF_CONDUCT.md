# 行为准则 / Code of Conduct

本项目采用 [Contributor Covenant v2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
的基本约定，摘要如下：

- **对事不对人。** 技术分歧用代码、测试和可复现的实验说话。
- **不欢迎**：人身攻击、歧视性言论、骚扰、泄露他人隐私（包括聊天记录、IP、真实姓名）。
- **欢迎**：指出别人的 bug、指出我的 bug、把复现步骤写清楚、把"我不确定"说出来。

维护者有权删除不当内容并拒绝参与。违规可以通过仓库的私密报告通道联系维护者。

---

We follow the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant v2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/):

- **Critique the code, not the person.** Settle technical disagreements with code, tests and
  reproducible experiments.
- **Not welcome**: personal attacks, discriminatory language, harassment, or publishing
  someone else's private data (chat logs, IPs, legal names).
- **Welcome**: pointing out bugs — including the maintainer's — with clear reproduction steps,
  and saying "I'm not sure".

Maintainers may remove inappropriate content and refuse participation.
Report violations privately through the repository's Security advisory channel.
